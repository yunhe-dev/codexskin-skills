# Asset handling and publishing

Use material supplied by the user for local creation, validation, preview, and
packaging without making legal conclusions. Record source and transformation
notes in the README.

Before transmitting a package, ask the user to confirm they may publish every
included asset. Do not infer permission from local possession, and do not upload
unless requested. Dream Skin resources are excluded unless the user separately
and explicitly requests them.

Packages must not include secrets, personal paths, private screenshots,
credentials, tracking, or unrelated source material. A gallery preview must
show the themed workspace, not only the raw artwork.
